Word order,
inversie & vragen

Hoofdzinnen with time – object – manner – place, inversion when the sentence starts with something else, and both kinds of question — closed and open — with the interrogative pronouns.

1 · Hoofdzin — every sentence that starts with a subject

subjectverbrest = time – object – manner – place

The rest always runs in the order time – object – manner – place (TOMP). elke morgen = time, de kinderen = object, met de auto = manner, naar school = place.

2 · Hoofdzin met inversie — the verb jumps in front of the subject

Inversie is when the subject and the verb switch places, so the verb comes before the subject. You get that whenever the sentence starts with something taken from the rest.

3 · Gesloten vraag — answer is ja or nee

verbsubjectrest (time – object – manner – place)

4 · Open vraag — starts with a vraagwoord

vraagwoordverbsubjectrest (time – object – manner – place)
Rules to remember
  1. Hoofdzin = subject – verb – rest; the rest is always time – object – manner – place.
  2. Inversie: put anything from the rest in first position and the verb still stays in second position, so the subject slides behind it: Elke morgen breng ik…, not Elke morgen ik breng…
  3. Gesloten vraag = verb – subject – rest. Open vraag = vraagwoord – verb – subject – rest.
  4. When jij / je comes after the verb, the -t disappears: jij drinkt → drink je?, jij doet → doe je?
  5. wie and wat as the subject of the question take the hij/zij form: Wie brengt de kinderen naar school?
  6. hoe can be combined with an adjective: hoe duur, hoe laat, hoeveel.
  7. welk goes with het-words (welk boek), welke with de-words (welke auto).
  8. the = de / het  ·  a/an = een.
